This Year

What to expect in 2026

The 2026 Medical Device Make-A-Thon brings interdisciplinary student teams together for a 72-hour sprint to conceptualize, design, and prototype solutions to a real clinical challenge; with workshops, mentorship, and fabrication resources along the way.

Full details on this year's problem statement, keynote speakers, mentors, judges, and weekend schedule will be published here as they are finalized.

No prior medical device experience is required; just curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to build.
